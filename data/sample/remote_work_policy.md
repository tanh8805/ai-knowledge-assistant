# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their three-month probation period may work remotely
up to three days per week. Interns may work remotely one day per week with their
mentor's approval.

## Core hours
Everyone must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on working days.
Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours whenever possible.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office budget of 300 USD.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of purchase to be reimbursed.

## Security
Company data may only be accessed through the VPN. Public Wi-Fi without the VPN
is not allowed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
